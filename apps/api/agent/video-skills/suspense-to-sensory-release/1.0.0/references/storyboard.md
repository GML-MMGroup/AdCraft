# Framing the change in understanding

Use contained medium views and selective close-ups for the approved setup.
Tighten framing as a clue becomes consequential, keeping eyelines and room
orientation consistent. Show the speaker's ordinary evidence, not a literal
supernatural event that the later demonstration cannot explain.

Choose the reveal frame first: its composition must expose the withheld
source or context quickly. Repeat a shape, contact point, or movement
direction from an earlier frame to make the connection legible. Keep labels,
wide views, or recognizable silhouettes out of setup shots only where the
approved information order requires it; source assets remain complete.

After the hinge, favor readable action-result pairs and product details over
unrelated beauty inserts. Build space for the dry callback and brand hold.
Use the approved sequence and shot count; a single local segment need not
repeat the full suspense-to-release arc.
