# Product Promotional Film

## Defining character

Use selling-point hierarchy, use context, benefit reveal and clean packshot logic
to make the approved commercial message easy to understand. Favor practical
visibility of operation and result. New Product TVC Campaign emphasizes launch
imagery and a recurring visual signature; this treatment emphasizes product-use
readability. Either can support several creative mechanisms.

## Coverage and information

Assign prominence to the main supported benefit, then use details or comparisons
for secondary information when the brief includes them. Choose camera side,
framing and focus that reveal the relevant contact or result without distorting
use posture or forcing every surface to face the lens.

Use context may be established by a gesture, environment, spoken line or prior
scene. Apply benefit reveal at the story's chosen point; a practical look does
not require an opening problem, failed attempt and solution in every film.
The product can be held, used, discussed or observed according to the concept.

## Finish and sound

Clean packshot logic means legible form, clear information hierarchy and sufficient
recognition time wherever display is planned. The whole-film ending belongs only
in its designated segment. Use precise operating sounds and intelligible speech;
music and exact graphic finishing follow their assigned production roles.
Preserve supplied geometry, claims and offers across demonstration and story shots.
