# Environment treatment

Use a quiet warm tabletop or workshop-like setting when the brief permits:
fine wood grain, muted tan or cocoa backgrounds, a soft directional light,
and spacious warm shadows. Preserve the specified location and architecture.

Build depth with a defined near surface, a usable middle plane, and a calm
rear plane. Keep support surfaces continuous and their roughness believable.
A broad bright area can motivate side illumination without an ornate backdrop.

Describe only the environment in this role. Leave the main display zone
visually quiet and reserve negative space where the brief needs it. Fine
atmosphere should remain sparse enough to keep geometry and materials clear.
