Use readable action geography, motivated camera movement, and continuity across shots.
Dialogue, ambient sound, Foley, and synchronous action sound may support the scene.
Do not request background music from the video provider; music remains a separate Audio task.

## Optional shot phrasing

When camera motion and subject action are being confused, establish the view or camera
path before describing the action within it. Use "while" for simultaneous motion and
"then" for actual sequence. Clarify toward or away from the lens, screen-left or screen-
right only where useful, keeping body-relative direction distinct. This is an optional
phrasing tool: preserve approved chronology, cuts, stillness, medium and rhythm rather
than enforcing a universal shot template.
