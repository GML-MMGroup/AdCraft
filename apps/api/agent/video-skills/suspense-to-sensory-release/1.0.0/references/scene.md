# Environmental appearance

For a suspense environment, favor limited practical light, restrained color,
quiet negative space, and clear planes of foreground and background. Keep
materials and major spatial features visible. Use the approved room type;
a workplace, home, shop, or service setting can carry this look without
becoming a clinic or interrogation room.

For a release environment, use clearer fill, natural surface color, and a
welcoming or precise atmosphere appropriate to the approved location. Warm timber is
one option, not a requirement. Preserve established geography when both
states belong to the same place. Environment references describe architecture,
lighting, and materials with coherent scale and usable spatial depth.
