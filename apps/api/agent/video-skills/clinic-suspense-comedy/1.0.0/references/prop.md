# Quiet supporting objects

Render approved supporting objects with believable scale, clear edges and
restrained material color. Documents, writing tools or routine room objects
can suggest everyday work when already present in the design. Do not add
syringes, restraint equipment or ominous devices just to signal a clinic.

If an object participates in a consultation gesture, preserve its shape,
orientation and distinctive details. Keep reference views complete and evenly
lit. Keep consultation objects ordinary and unbranded; they should not disguise
the advertised item as a supporting object before its final appearance.

For an approved functional insert, supporting mechanisms and affected surfaces
may be designed as needed. Preserve their real geometry and materials; verify
their role against the brief so the item being sold is not reclassified as
an ordinary prop to expose it early.
