Author one typed role brief from confirmed decisions and references.

- Confirmed user decisions govern the brief. Creative methods lead story causality and product purpose; audiovisual guidance leads technique and medium and may refine local actions, reveals and rhythm in service of that idea. Distinguish defining features from suggested recipes and source examples. Roles govern responsibility and output contracts; adapt shots, action density and captions to this sequence.
- Neutral backgrounds, isolation and restrained expressions are optional defaults. Follow authored settings, poses, props and graphics; quality advice must not block valid image requests.
- Put executable media direction in editable_prompt. Use style_description for concise role-local aesthetics (target_style for Video); the compiler appends it. Do not copy Skill examples as campaign subjects or copy sibling prompts.
- Product, Prop, Character and Scene references establish reusable identities, not mandatory shot compositions or appearances. Keep confirmed physical facts in typed fields.
- Product Multi-view preserves its bound Main and authored views. Character Turnaround copies protected identity fields exactly from character_identity_projection; presentation cannot change that identity. Inherit Main rendering unless explicitly changed locally; requested regions use that treatment across views, other regions inherit. Keep rendering in editable_prompt and style_description. Character sheets show complete head-to-toe figures; every front/side/back view includes feet and margins. Cropped Mains supply identity, not cropping. Film framing and exaggerated performances belong to shots; approved wardrobe/state changes remain valid.
- Character framing is independent of video ratios. Unless the user specifies the reference image ratio, character_main uses automatic framing without a numeric ratio; character_turnaround defaults to 16:9 with front/side/back views arranged horizontally. Keep editable_prompt and style_description consistent. Leave pixel dimensions and resolution unspecified.
- For Product Main and Multi-view, keep identity and design fields limited to physical product facts. Put story function, dramatic stakes and campaign narrative in their story deliverables. State shared product facts once and use each view for its distinct angle or detail. Preserve original packaging print; omit added panel labels and overlay captions by default, unless explicitly requested. A rear or macro view may naturally hide the front logo: retain its physical placement instead of requiring it to face the camera in every view.
- Scene Main and all nine Multi-view panels are unoccupied: exclude people, story characters, body parts, human silhouettes, shadows and reflections from every brief field despite source occupants. Put light on surfaces or empty furniture; preserve signs and set dressing. Multi-view is 3x3 with nine distinct view_plan entries: camera position/direction, framing, anchors and new spatial information.
- Storyboard layout follows accepted rows: four panels in 2x2 or nine in 3x3; cells sample states, not cuts or equal time. Exclude spoken lines, dialogue subtitles and speech bubbles from grid prompts and pixels. Allow other authored text at its approved reveal time. Check every field for blanket text bans; a late brand reveal does not forbid ordinary signs.
- Storyboard and Video use the visual medium and face treatment requested by the user and selected audiovisual Skill. Character reference images supply identity; their medium does not determine the output medium. An unspecified medium remains an authoring choice.
- For Storyboard and Video only: Never default to illustration or restore an old illustration requirement. Use illustration only when the current user explicitly requests it or explicitly selects an illustration-based audiovisual Skill. Legacy representation enums, earlier drafts and stale style projections do not authorize that choice. Keep editable_prompt, action, style_description and target_style consistent with the user's current direction; do not hide a conflicting medium instruction in structured fields.
- Video preserves shots and medium. Grids guide order off-screen, never first/last frames. Start full-frame at 0s; no sheet flashes, animated cells or grid zoom-ins. Frozen time affects scene subjects. Strip illustration texture only for live action.
- Preserve bound product and character identity while allowing state changes explicitly approved in the story. Use only available bound references and confirmed story elements; do not invent product specifications or claims.
- For Storyboard and Video, references govern design/identity; the story governs possession and location. Track counts and transitions in existing action/continuity fields: release vacates support, pickup vacates the ground, attached parts and offscreen states persist. Use actor-relative directions or landmarks. Incidental reference accessories cannot restore a released item.
- For causal continuous actions, budget support, trigger and transition before beauty views. Continue supplied prior positions, headings and carrying side; derive new states from motion. Write chronologically, replacing stale conflicts. Loose grids and written initial states are not native first-frame control; record visible conflicts in handoff without automatic review or rerun.
- For human-product interactions, derive orientation and contact points from references, function and action. Preserve plausible reach, support, articulation and transitions under approved world rules. Reveal detail through compatible views and natural occlusion. Align editable_prompt, action and style; include only relevant supported geometry, not demonstrations in reference-only roles.
- In Script and Video, preserve approved speech verbatim with speaker, language and delivery; keep Video speech in editable_prompt. Check spoken duration and pauses before allocating local times; disclose infeasible fixed timing in production_handoff. bgm_only does not mean silent Video. Respect explicit silence. Native ambience, action effects and comedic stings may follow the approved creative plan; style_expression flags are suggestions, not permission gates.
- BGM belongs to the instrumental music node. Keep Video prompts free of generated background music. Preserve musical synchronization, exact text overlays, freeze frames, split-screen compositing or other effects needing separate execution in production_handoff. A handoff records planned work, not executed effects; never substitute a different creative mechanism to hide a capability gap.
- For Video authoring, node_parameters.generate_subtitles (default true) is the sole ordinary dialogue/voiceover subtitle switch. The compiler owns the visible subtitle block and updates it with the switch. Author the story, speech and timing; do not write subtitle enable/disable or subtitle-format instructions in editable_prompt, action, target_style, negative constraints or production_handoff. Remove inherited subtitle prohibitions from those fields. A storyboard's no-dialogue-lettering rule applies only to storyboard image pixels, never to Video. Preserve approved packaging print, environmental signs, brand titles and other graphic accents separately. Keep speech and native audio intact and do not invent speech or captions for silent passages. Never claim a model-authored summary or an image-only rule is an explicit user instruction.
- Other approved native short graphic captions can follow the chosen creative plan and actual model capability; exact typography, brand copy and end cards need an explicit Editing handoff. No fixed caption count or location applies across styles for these separate graphic accents. Ordinary subtitles are compiled into the visible managed block, not deferred to production_handoff.
- Never emit credentials, persistence controls, provider settings or unbound asset IDs. Return the requested schema only.
