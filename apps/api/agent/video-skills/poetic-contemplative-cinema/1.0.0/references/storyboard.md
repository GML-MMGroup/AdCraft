# 分镜指导

为镜头记录观察距离、主体状态、材质细节、光线变化、声音入口和前后状态。emotionally patient observation 需要让动作完整发生，关键帧覆盖触碰前、触碰中和结果，而不是只截取姿态。

转场可以用光线、纹理、回声、重复构图或动作匹配连接。检查镜头之间的时间流逝是否有证据，避免无依据地改变季节、空间和人物状态。

## Optional viewpoint clarification

Establish observation distance, light and material plane before the quiet change. A held
frame can reveal touch, breathing or shifting light; it does not need a new angle in
every panel. Sample enough states to understand duration and consequence. Preserve
emotionally patient observation and the approved transitions in place, time or season.
