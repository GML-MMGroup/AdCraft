skill_kind: audiovisual_style
title: High-End Fashion Film
objective: >
  Premium, restrained, generous with negative space, and read as a fashion
  film rather than a product advertisement.
visual_principles: >
  Low information density, strong composition, large areas of negative space,
  restrained performance, material and light before detail, and very little
  explanatory text.
camera: >
  Stable, slow, controlled movement. Medium close-ups and close-ups carry the
  piece; avoid heavy handheld motion.
product_treatment: >
  Do not rush a hard product display. When the product appears it must be
  accurate, and the hero shot may be stiller than everything around it.
editing: >
  Slow escalation with a small number of strong beats; do not rely on fast
  jump cuts.
sound: >
  Minimal score, strong ambience, deliberate silence, and amplified product
  sound.
suitable_cases: >
  Fragrance, fashion, jewellery, automotive, and premium skincare.
---

# High-End Fashion Film

The style earns its premium read from what it withholds. Composition and light
do the work that copy and cutaways would otherwise do.
