# Abstract Form Language

The commercial is a short piece of visual music. Nothing is explained, yet
every form moves with the character the brand wants to own.

## Why It Works

Form is read faster than words. When the motion of a shape already feels like
the product — light, precise, liquid, calm — the reveal confirms a feeling the
audience has been having for the whole film.

## How To Execute

1. Name the brand feeling as a physical quality: weight, speed, temperature, or texture.
2. Choose one primary form and one material that embody that quality.
3. Define the rules of motion and keep every shot inside them.
4. Lock sound to motion so rhythm, not narration, carries the structure.
5. Resolve the final motion into the product so the abstraction has an answer.
