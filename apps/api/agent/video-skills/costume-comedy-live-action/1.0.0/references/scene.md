# Tangible ceremonial space

Build one coherent period interior with a clear central axis, lacquer-red
architectural accents, selectively gilded detail, and warm directional light.
Stone or timber floors should support perspective and believable reflections.
Use layered depth and physical ornament instead of painted flatness.

Keep proportions, doors, columns, steps, and illumination consistent across
the requested empty-environment views. A palace hall is the source example;
a household interior or academy can use the same formal visual organization
at a smaller scale. Avoid covering the floor with decorative clouds or
filling every background surface with identical gold dragons.
