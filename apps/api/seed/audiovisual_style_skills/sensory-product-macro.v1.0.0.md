skill_id: sensory-product-macro
version: 1.0.0
skill_kind: audiovisual_style
title: Sensory Product Macro
objective: >
  Let the viewer almost feel the material of the product.
visual_principles: >
  Texture, surface and micro-detail lead. Light is shaped to reveal material
  rather than to decorate the frame.
camera: >
  Extreme macro work on liquid, surface, mist, gloss, friction and small
  changes, with shallow depth of field and precise focus pulls.
product_treatment: >
  Emphasise material, structure, touch and detail; the product is shown as a
  physical object rather than a label.
editing: >
  Match the rhythm of the material: unhurried holds, then a short accent on
  the decisive physical change.
sound: >
  Spray, friction, clicks, liquid, packaging open and close, and ASMR detail.
suitable_cases: >
  Fragrance, skincare, food, beverages, footwear, and electronics.
---

# Sensory Product Macro

Every shot answers one question about the material. If a frame does not add
touch information, it does not belong in the sequence.
