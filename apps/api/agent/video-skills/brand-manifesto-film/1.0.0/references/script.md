# Voice and symbolic emphasis

Translate the confirmed brand belief into a voice, image motif and emotional
rhythm. Belief-led narration may be poetic or direct; ground factual statements
in supplied evidence. Symbolic imagery should reinforce an established meaning
without inventing a character's history or replacing the chosen mechanism.

Use repetition, widening perspective, pauses or vocal intensity to shape
emotional escalation where the story calls for it. A quiet or nonlinear structure
can also express conviction. Brand resolution should preserve the approved
takeaway rather than force a new hero story or universal crescendo.
