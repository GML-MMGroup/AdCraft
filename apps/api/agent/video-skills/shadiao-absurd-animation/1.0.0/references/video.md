# Readable expressive deformation

Use graphic two-dimensional animation with bold contours and flat color adapted
to the approved cast. Make each planned squash, stretch or abrupt transformation
express its force or emotional cause. Follow it with the approved resulting state;
rebound is optional when transformation is meant to persist.

Contrast energetic movement with a held pose or ordinary response where useful.
Retain dialogue and other expressions that the approved story needs. A segment
can contain multiple actions and shots. Optional comic captions and synchronous
effects follow the local beat and actual capability; exact wording and typography
remain in the finishing handoff. The BGM role owns music. Keep all character
identities and the supplied product recognizable through stylization.
