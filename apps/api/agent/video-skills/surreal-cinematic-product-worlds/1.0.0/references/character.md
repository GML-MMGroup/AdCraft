# One identity across changing worlds

Use the approved cast size and independent identities. Carry facial or head
features, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe, and assigned accessories
through a threshold. A new background or lighting state does not create a new
cast member or change who owns a foreground hand.

Choose an understandable opening state and a performable response: curiosity,
surprise, calm delight, confidence, or another approved emotion. Exaggeration
can be expressive while anatomy, eyelines, and contact remain coherent. The
reference's tired-to-pleased office performance is only one possible arc.

Track orientation and posture during a turn or camera reversal so the same
body is not left behind at its previous position. A deliberate identity
transformation requires explicit story direction; ordinary visual effects
should not change identities incidentally.

Reference assets use their assigned image treatment and format. This style
adds no blanket illustrated-face rule and does not replace an explicitly
approved nonhuman identity with a human.
