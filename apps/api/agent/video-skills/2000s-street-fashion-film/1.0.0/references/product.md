# Wearable identity and material

Record the supplied item's silhouette, scale, proportions, visible materials,
seams, fastenings, hardware, pattern placement, and authorized branding. Preserve
actual hues beneath the warm daylight and cooler shade; an era-inspired grade
must not recolor the merchandise.

For garments, emphasize cut, layered edges, stitching, and believable fabric
weight. For eyewear, preserve frame geometry, bridge, temples, and lens appearance.
For bags, preserve body shape, handle attachment, strap length, grain, and
closure. Choose the appropriate features instead of transferring leather or
denim to every category.

Use a clear asset presentation that exposes those facts. Motion-oriented uses
are later coverage choices, not a reason to turn the identity asset into an
unrelated street vignette. Infer hidden surfaces conservatively; do not turn
an attractive highlight into a claim about material composition or performance.
