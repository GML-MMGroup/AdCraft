# Sunlit Pastel Product Fantasy

Give commercial imagery the bright, delightful atmosphere of a beautifully
built leisure destination. The finish combines believable surfaces and clear
daylight with playful scale and gently impossible motion. Apply this treatment
to the approved concept; its story can involve travel, discovery, display, or
another action appropriate to the brief.

## Color and light

Use warm cream or a pale brand-related hue as the main environmental color,
one blush or powder-toned accent, and open sky blue where the setting allows.
Keep the approved subject color exact, including when it is dark or saturated.
Let pale surroundings support that contrast. Reserve small dark areas for
structural depth and graphic definition.

Use one clear daylight direction with broad sky fill, gently softened shadow
edges, and controlled highlights. Preserve volume in pale surfaces and detail
in reflective areas. Avoid a blanket yellow wash, blown-out whites, dense fog,
or nightclub color contamination.

## Materials and space

Differentiate satin lacquer, transparent glass, polished or brushed metal,
painted architectural panels, and organic surfaces. Reflections, refraction,
contact shading, and consistent light make the fantasy feel constructed.
Avoid coating every surface in the same plastic gloss or turning everything
into clay-like animation.

Build depth with a nearby framing edge, a clear middle-distance subject zone,
and a readable distant horizon. Rounded architecture, open plazas, curved
paths, and restrained ornamental structures suit the treatment. Choose the
location from the brief; specific attractions or city landmarks are optional.

## Framing and movement

Favor spacious perspective with a controlled wide-lens feel. Keep the main
silhouette away from extreme frame-edge stretching and retain enough depth
of field to understand the geography. Strong parallax can sell movement while
the subject remains easy to follow.

For moving shots, use smooth dolly-like travel, low sweeping approaches, or a
modest orbit with one dominant direction. Accelerate and settle gradually.
Preserve screen direction and spatial anchors through foreground occlusions
or hidden joins. Let background motion support the central action; avoid
constant spinning, frantic zooming, or simultaneous movement everywhere.

## Commercial finish

Build visual energy toward a clear closing composition, then allow a stable
recognition beat. Keep the main form crisp against a quieter background.
Use decorative elements sparingly so the result remains polished and airy.
Typography and overlays follow the approved brief; source-video tutorial
panels and reference thumbnails are not part of this treatment.

Role-specific guidance in the manifest supplies material, environment,
storyboard, moving-image, and instrumental-score decisions. Production
parameters and internal capability instructions retain their existing authority.
