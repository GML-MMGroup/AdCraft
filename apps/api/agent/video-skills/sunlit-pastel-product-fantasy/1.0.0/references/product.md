# Product material and identity

Preserve the supplied silhouette, proportions, closure, pump or cap, seams,
color, and approved printed artwork. A cream-colored setting does not require
recoloring the package. Keep physical features and typography stable across
angles; use larger apparent presentation through framing when appropriate.

Separate material responses: broad satin highlights on the body, narrower
controlled highlights on metal, and credible thickness and refraction on
transparent parts. Show enough edge and contact shading to retain volume.
Protect printed artwork from glare. Avoid adding a clear shell, liquid fill,
metallic finish, or rounded corners absent from the supplied design.

For the identity asset, use the isolated presentation required by the current
role, a restrained neutral or pale backdrop, and a clean daylight-inspired
lighting setup. The approved geometry remains the source for later fantasy
staging. End with a design description specific enough to recognize the same
object in subsequent views.

Good: a satin body, brushed-metal closure, crisp artwork, and a soft grounding
shadow. Poor: a uniformly glossy object whose closure and lettering change
between views.
