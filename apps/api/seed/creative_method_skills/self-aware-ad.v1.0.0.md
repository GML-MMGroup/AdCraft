# Self-Aware Ad

The commercial drops the act. By admitting it is an ad, it earns enough trust
for its one real point to be believed.

## Why It Works

Audiences are trained to resist advertising. An ad that names its own tricks
disarms that resistance and makes the brand feel honest and confident.

## How To Execute

1. List the clichés of the category the audience is tired of.
2. Choose the one truthful product point that survives without them.
3. Break the frame early and directly.
4. Keep the tone warm toward the viewer, not superior.
5. Close with the honest point and a line that keeps the self-awareness.
