# Mode launcher prototype

This is a throwaway composition study for the two creation modes. It answers: does a full-screen split stage with a clapperboard hinge make the choice feel deliberate and directional?

The divider geometry is intentionally vertical: its upper hinge is the fixed pivot, and the black and white chevrons open around that pivot. The selected panel leaves in the same direction as the opened divider.

- Open `/design-lab/mode-launcher` in the web app.
- Use the dock to compare `cinema`, `archive`, and `signal`.
- Click either panel to preview the one-way launch. The prototype does not create a project or navigate to a workflow.

The production version should keep the validated visual decisions while extracting the stage, mode panel, icon, clapper divider, and transition into focused components shared by the home and projects entry points.

The mode symbols use explicit semantics: Personal Creation is an individual idea trail ending in a spark inside an open frame; Brand Professional is a modular identity mark aligned to a brand-system grid with registration nodes.

The blade is intentionally longer than the viewport so it stays visually continuous during travel. The vertical blade, its top hinge, and the selected panel share one horizontal travel clock; there is no blade rotation. The other panel is hidden against the black stage. Reduced motion fades in place.
