# Readable identity and emotional contrast

Favor recognizable silhouettes, stable hair or species features, distinct
wardrobe values, and expressive eyes and brows appropriate to the chosen
design. Social contrast can come from posture and clothing without assigning
fixed moral traits to gender, body type, wealth, or species.

Build the requested identity view with a calm, consistent baseline. Save
accusation, shock, resolve, and sales confidence for performance coverage;
do not bake a permanent scream or extreme pose into reference views. Preserve
the same identity through the switch from wounded restraint to composure.

Follow the selected rendering treatment. Non-human casts retain their own
anatomy and surface style. Local treatment of realistic human facial regions
does not dictate the rendering of hair, costume, surroundings, or every other
cast member. Keep the current image-role composition and text-free contract.
