# Everyday pulse, spatial release, and a clear landing

Propose an original instrumental arc with a restrained everyday opening, a
brief change at the threshold, greater space or warmth after arrival, and a
controlled ending beneath the product view. The contrast may be harmonic,
textural, or rhythmic; it does not require a loud impact or an epic crescendo.

Choose timbres for the brand and desired feeling. Dry percussion and a narrow
texture can open into sustained notes, a broader bass space, or airy accents.
These are alternatives, not a fixed genre or an identification of the source
recording. A playful, premium, or contemplative brief can use the same arc.

Give tactile use sounds and any dialogue enough room. Let the closing phrase
resolve rather than introduce another transformation. Fit the approved runtime
and audio mode. Exact transition synchronization is an editing intention, not
an already completed mix; no source melody or unverified tempo is prescribed.
