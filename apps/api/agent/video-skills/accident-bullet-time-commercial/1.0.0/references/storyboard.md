# Stage the cause before the frozen-space tour

Use the approved inventory, visit order and physical map. Keep the first-frame
footwear reveal through all objects landing in one video segment and its matching
grid. Reserve landing time within the approved duration. No separate setup node.

Stage causal samples first. For the usual nine-panel incident, allocate:
1. First-frame footwear-level opening: feet on the ground, low backward tracking;
   belongings remain supported even when outside this crop.
2. The same camera rises to reveal the wearer, walking routes and initial support
   before contact: items visibly held or worn.
3. Incidental passing contact, slight backward recoil, opened palms and the
   original items beginning to rise away as one quick causal reaction.
4. Shared airborne freeze: that pose, empty supports and item depths legible.
5-7. One dominant detail visit per item, following the approved order.
8. Continuous pullback reconnecting the same frozen space.
9. All released items grounded and the approved reaction.
These are unequal-time samples, not nine cuts. Prioritize ambiguous transitions
over repeated beauty angles. Depict contact; a caption is insufficient.
