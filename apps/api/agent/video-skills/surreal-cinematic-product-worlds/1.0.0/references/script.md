# Coherent wonder in the approved narrative

Use spatial contrast, scale cues, tactile detail and motivated transitions to
make the selected idea legible. A product-triggered world can begin in ordinary
life; a visual metaphor may begin inside the impossible world and reveal the
product later. Preserve the mechanism's information order and product role.

Choose anticipation, discovery, reaction and sensory pauses for the actual story.
When a threshold or use action occurs, make its cause and resulting state clear.
Cast, setting, dialogue and the return to ordinary scale follow confirmed choices.
A material detail can connect the world to the item without adding a compulsory
demonstration. Keep factual claims and exact finishing wording grounded in the brief.
