# Shot and focus design

Alternate a material detail with enough context to explain the relevant
shape or action. For a miniature concept, pair a close view of the contact
point with a wider scale anchor; the figure and tool should share a usable
focus zone. Avoid blurring the mechanism that explains the shot.

Specify the dominant focal plane, light direction, near occluder if present,
and the same surface feature that will anchor the following view. Use ridges,
layer boundaries, or grain direction to preserve geography. Match direction
and surface state across cuts rather than assuming every move is continuous.

Keep the final composition wide and calm enough to recognize the complete
object. Preserve layer order and the approved display surface. Reserve clear
brand space when requested, away from complex texture and bright dust.
