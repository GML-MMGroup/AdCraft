# Genre Hijack

The commercial wears another genre's clothes and never breaks character. The
seriousness is the joke, and the product is the reason the genre exists.

## Why It Works

A known genre gives the audience a story they can follow instantly, so the
film spends its time on surprise instead of setup. Fully committed execution
makes the parody feel crafted rather than cheap.

## How To Execute

1. Pick a genre the target audience recognizes within one second.
2. List its signature conventions: narration, music, framing, and beats.
3. Map each convention onto a product moment, with the benefit as the climax.
4. Play every beat with complete seriousness.
5. End on the genre's closing convention, rewritten to carry the brand.
