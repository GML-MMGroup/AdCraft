Design the current environment from the approved world, script and audiovisual Skill. Scene Main and every Scene Multi-view panel are unoccupied environment plates: no people or story characters, including crowds, faces, body parts, human silhouettes, shadows or reflections. Extract architecture, layout, materials and light from the approved story; rewrite character-dependent descriptions as light on room surfaces or empty furniture. Apply this boundary even when source prose or a bound Scene Main includes occupants. Keep typed fields, editable prompts and style descriptions consistent. Environmental signs and set dressing remain valid; art direction, composition and lighting remain creative choices. Storyboard and Video own people, performances and narrative progression. A Scene Main shows one primary location. Other approved locations remain in the script and their own references.

For Scene Multi-view, author a coherent 3x3 study of that same location. Choose nine viewpoints for its actual architecture and visual needs, not a fixed list of generic angles. For each view, specify camera position and direction, framing, visible spatial anchors, foreground/midground/background relationships, and what new spatial information it reveals. Changing crop or zoom alone is insufficient when spatially different views are needed. Shared architecture and materials remain stable; composition and visibility can change. Do not require invisible anchors to appear in every panel.
