# A few useful supporting objects

Choose only supporting items needed for the approved occasion: a cooler,
towel, simple glass, chair, sports accessory, or everyday tabletop object.
A functional prop should help explain retrieval, rest, contact, or use rather
than become a second advertised item.

Give materials believable summer behavior: cloth absorbs light differently
from wet glass, ice remains solid within a plausible cooling setup, and
paper does not acquire a plastic shine. Preserve scale and stable construction
in the assigned reference format. Brand marks and specific ingredients come
from approved assets rather than decoration.

Ice, sliced fruit, palm leaves, and beach equipment are optional source details.
A quiet indoor routine can use ordinary household objects instead.
