# Emotional Micro-Moment Amplification

The commercial takes one unglamorous, true instant and treats it with the
attention usually reserved for spectacle.

## Why It Works

Specificity is the shortcut to recognition. A moment filmed precisely enough
feels remembered, not invented.

## How To Execute

1. Pick a moment specific enough to exclude people, which sharpens recognition
   for the right audience.
2. Amplify exactly one gesture, sound, or expression.
3. Let the viewer complete the feeling before the product appears.
4. Keep the product present but quiet.
5. Resist resolution; end inside the feeling.
