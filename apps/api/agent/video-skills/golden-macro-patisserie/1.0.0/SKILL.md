# Golden Macro Patisserie

Make small material details feel rich, intimate, and appetizing. Combine warm
amber light, tactile microtexture, selective focus, and restrained movement.
Apply the treatment to the approved story; miniature staging is compatible
when already present in the concept, but is not required by this style.

## Palette and illumination

Build the palette from toasted gold, butter cream, warm wood, and a muted tan
or cocoa background. Dark indigo fruit can supply a concentrated cool accent
when appropriate to the supplied subject. Preserve actual ingredient and
brand colors rather than tinting everything orange.

Use a soft directional side or rear key with restrained fill. Let grazing
light describe raised edges and layered surfaces. Keep creamy whites neutral
enough to read as fresh, dark accents textured, and glossy areas below broad
clipping. Fine backlit particles can catch the light when justified by the
scene, without becoming constant glitter or fog.

## Material separation

Use crisp flaky edges, small fractures, and uneven baked ridges for pastry;
soft porous or piped structure for cream; and a restrained waxy bloom with
small highlights for fruit. Preserve differences between dry powder, moist
filling, warm glaze, wood grain, and brushed metal. Match these responses to
the actual materials rather than applying one universal shine.

The subject should feel edible or touchable, not rubbery, metallic, or coated
in thick plastic. Small imperfections support richness; burned surfaces,
greasy pooling, and excessive debris can reduce appeal.

## Optical character and finish

Use selective focus to isolate the relevant surface while leaving enough
recognizable structure to understand its shape. Favor a soft warm background
and gentle highlight falloff. Avoid heavy edge blur that makes the central
form look like a flat cutout. Widen the usable focus for a complete-object
presentation so the silhouette and major layers stay legible.

Compose around a clear material focal point with quieter negative space.
Keep decorative detail subordinate to the approved subject. Preserve the
specified representation and identity; this style adds lighting, texture,
and optical character without forcing a particular story or rendering medium.

Role references provide the relevant asset, environment, shot, movement, or
instrumental-score guidance. Project parameters and internal capability rules
retain their existing authority.
