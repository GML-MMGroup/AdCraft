# Synesthetic Translation

The commercial shows the inside of a sensation. One sense is made visible,
and the audience recognizes it from their own memory.

## Why It Works

Sensory claims are hard to believe in words. A translation that matches what
people already associate with a taste or sound skips the claim and triggers
the memory directly.

## How To Execute

1. Isolate the single sensation that defines the product or variant.
2. Map it to color, temperature, texture, and motion that match common associations.
3. Trigger the translation at the exact moment of contact.
4. Escalate the sensory world to one peak rather than a sequence of effects.
5. Return to the real moment so the product owns the sensation.

This method differs from Visual Metaphor: a metaphor invents a world rule for
an abstract value, while a translation renders a concrete sensation.
