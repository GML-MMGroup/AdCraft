# Motif and emotional emphasis

Sample the approved sequence in the required nine-panel grid. Choose symbolic
imagery, repeated composition, scale or light to reinforce its established
meaning. A motif should develop through the selected actions, not introduce a
new story or a different brand claim.

Give a planned emotional turn preparation and perceptible change. Brand resolution
belongs at its designated point in the whole film. Preserve identities, eyelines,
product facts and local ending states while choosing coverage for the actual
mechanism; every grid need not build to a new climax.
