# 分镜指导

先用建立镜头说明动作地理，再按中景动作、局部接触、反应和结果切换。为关键帧记录起点、攻击线、落脚点、兵器方向、衣摆惯性和换位后的关系。每次剪切都要能恢复双方位置。

用同向移动、衣摆遮挡、地形线或兵器交接连接镜头。检查特效是否遮挡接触点和重心变化，避免只展示姿态。

## Optional viewpoint clarification

Set the camera side, terrain and movement line before the approved step, encounter or
attack. Track landing, weapon direction and garment inertia at relevant states. A wide
geography view, contact insert and reaction may be separate shots. Do not invent combat
or force a continuous orbit when the story calls for travel or a quiet gesture.
