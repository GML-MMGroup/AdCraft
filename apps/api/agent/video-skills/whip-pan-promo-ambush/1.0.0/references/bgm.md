# Expectation, reversal, and speech space

Propose an original instrumental structure that briefly supports the setup,
changes or pauses at the seller reveal, and leaves room for the direct pitch.
A tense pulse can turn into a lighter rhythmic bed; a domestic version can
use a small interruption of an otherwise ordinary mood.

The reveal does not require a fixed comedy sting, orchestral hit, or loud drop.
Choose an accent that fits the brand and leaves the first promotional words
clear. Keep density lower while prices, bundle terms, or conditions are spoken.
Resolve beneath a readable ending rather than launching another escalation.

Fit the approved 8-30 second creative range and actual audio mode. Dialogue,
weather, knocks, and contact sounds belong to their designated audio handoff.
Any frame-specific accent is an editing intention, not an already executed
mix. Source melody, exact instruments, and measured tempo were not established.
