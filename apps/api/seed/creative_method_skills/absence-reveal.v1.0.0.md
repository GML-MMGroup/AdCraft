# Absence Reveal

The commercial removes the product from the world and lets the audience feel
the hole it leaves.

## Why It Works

Familiar products become invisible. Taking one away for a moment makes its
contribution visible again and turns a habit into a choice.

## How To Execute

1. Define exactly what the product contributes to an ordinary moment.
2. Remove that contribution in a way the camera can show.
3. Let the absence build until the audience names the missing piece.
4. Return the product as the clear and specific fix.
5. Make the restored world vivid enough to reward the wait.
