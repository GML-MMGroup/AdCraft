  Follow one small product moment to its consequences, then the consequences

  Causal curiosity and escalation; each link answers what happens next, and
  the growing gap between a tiny cause and a large effect makes the benefit
  feel bigger than any direct claim.

  A small, specific product moment, the first direct consequence, a chain of
  increasingly larger and more surprising effects, the peak consequence, and a
  return to the original small moment.

  Open on a cause so small it seems insignificant, and cut to its first
  consequence fast enough that the audience senses a chain beginning.

# Consequence Chain

The commercial is a domino run. A single product moment tips the first piece,
and every link makes the benefit larger.

## Why It Works

Direct claims about magnitude are discounted. A chain lets the audience
calculate the magnitude themselves, one believable step at a time.

## How To Execute

1. Choose the smallest concrete product moment that starts the benefit.
2. Write every link as a plausible cause and effect.
3. Escalate scale, speed, or emotion with each link.
4. Stop the chain at the peak that still serves the benefit.
5. Return to the first moment so the product is remembered as the cause.
