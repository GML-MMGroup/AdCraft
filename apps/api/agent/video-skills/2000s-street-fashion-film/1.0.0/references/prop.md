# Supporting urban objects

Choose a few plausible neighborhood objects whose shape or material belongs
to the approved setting: a cafe chair, newspaper, bicycle, traffic fixture,
or simple street furnishing. Their weathering, metal, paper, and painted finish
should share the photographic daylight and moderate contrast of the campaign.

Give each needed object a stable shape and scale. Avoid adding conspicuous
third-party marks or fabricated campaign text. Hero merchandise retains its
own asset identity; a supporting object must not become a substitute version
of the advertised item. Present each required asset clearly in its assigned
reference format.
