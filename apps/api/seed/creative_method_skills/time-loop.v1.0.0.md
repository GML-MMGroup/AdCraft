# Time Loop

The commercial traps its hero in a repeated moment. Each loop teaches the
audience the moment better, so the product's change lands with full clarity.

## Why It Works

Repetition turns a moment into a puzzle the audience wants solved. The
product becomes the solution they were waiting for.

## How To Execute

1. Choose a short, recognizable moment that the product improves.
2. Establish the loop with an identical repeat early.
3. Vary one element per loop, escalating toward the product.
4. Keep the total number of loops small enough to stay fun.
5. Break the loop with the product and let time move forward.
