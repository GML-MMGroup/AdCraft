# Surreal Cinematic Product Worlds

Use coherent impossible space, motivated spatial transitions, tactile
product detail, and legible scale. Grounded-to-impossible staging and a settled
hero finish suit many briefs; the approved mechanism determines the entry
point, product timing and ending. Favor photographic surfaces and cinematic depth
as in the reference; an explicitly requested alternative medium retains its
own treatment. Surreal events do not automatically require illustrated faces.

## World contrast and light

Give the ordinary state a readable layout, natural materials, and a restrained
palette. Let the expanded state feel meaningfully different through depth,
light, atmosphere, scale, or material. The reference moves from cool office
tones to a warm golden landscape; that contrast is an example, not a required
blue-to-gold grade or a reason to recolor the actual package.

Preserve believable reflections, contact shading, translucency, fabric, and
surface detail within each state. A transition may change light visibly, but
the arrival needs one coherent lighting logic. Backlit particles, atmospheric
depth, or luminous boundaries should reveal form rather than wash out the hero.

## Transition grammar

Choose a clear bridge: a use gesture, aligned silhouette, opening, light sweep,
foreground wipe, or change of viewpoint. Match the owning identity, item,
screen direction, and relevant pose across the threshold. Reveal enough of
the departure and arrival to understand the spatial change.

Impossible scale needs a legible reference and a planned result. Keep the
supplied item's proportions and design recognizable while scale or context
changes. Floating surroundings and deliberate set transformations can express
the rule; uncontrolled warping of hands, faces, or packaging cannot substitute
for a directed effect. A product transfer must have a visible owner and path.

## Camera, material, and closure

For a product-triggered change, relational framing, contact detail and an
opening view can clarify the new space. For another approved mechanism,
choose coverage that preserves its own information order. A push, an arc, a reverse view, or a matched cut can
support that reveal without demanding one uninterrupted take. Let camera
movement and background activity have a clear focus instead of constant orbiting.

Tangible details such as food surface, liquid, cream, fabric, glass or a
device control can connect spectacle to the item. Use them where the concept
calls for them. When interaction is shown, keep its physical relationship
understandable; a separate use demonstration is optional.
The close can remain in the transformed setting; it need not revisit the
opening room. Quiet secondary movement and leave the actual branding readable.

## Scope and routing

Cast size follows the approved brief. Keep each cast identity and its assigned
item distinct through transitions and scale changes. Intermediate images follow
their own representation contracts. Typography and exact finishing remain
explicit handoff requirements. The intended 15-30 second format does not
override approved timing, frame shape, or provider limits.
