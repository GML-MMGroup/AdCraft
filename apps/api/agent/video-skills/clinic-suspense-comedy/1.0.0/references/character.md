# Distinct consultation identities

Make the approved doctor, nurse and patient visually distinguishable through
their existing face, hair, silhouette and wardrobe decisions. Medical workwear
should read naturally; patient clothing can be ordinary. Do not infer gender,
age, facial deformity or a mandatory disheveled appearance from these roles.

Preserve one stable identity per role across neutral references and expressive
shots. Keep turnaround views evenly readable in the selected medium. In
performance, eyes, mouth tension and posture can move from sincerity into
comic surprise or delight; exaggerated expressions are allowed without
changing the face or making all three react identically.

If a closing patient performance is selected, carry the approved distinctive smile,
gaze or gesture through this same identity. Convey the scripted state through
acting, not a newly distorted face; neutral design and turnaround references
do not need to permanently wear the closing expression.
