Revisit a settled decision only to identify a concrete conflict. Keep the speech-and-action timing check below, check continuity of actions, screen direction and character state across shots, and preserve the planned first product reveal and reversal timing. Narrative headings do not require separate nodes. For tight windows, include a brief speech + non-overlapping action time <= available time check inside the existing content field, using the same estimates as the shot schedule. Rebalance approximate boundaries or discretionary holds within confirmed constraints before compressing delivery.

A cut during a line or its audible tail must fall inside that stated audible interval; required reactions that extend past it create a timing conflict, not a successful check. Resolve the schedule or identify the conflicting requirements in the handoff. Keep the timecodes, timing notes and prose consistent rather than asserting that all timing fits.

Before returning, budget exact approved speech at the intended delivery pace, including pauses and sequential actions. Adjust only approximate local timing and discretionary holds within fixed segment windows. If fixed speech, action and timing cannot coexist, identify the specific conflict in the available review or production handoff; preserve the words and duration without claiming impossible subsecond timing is executable.
