# Merchandise that reads during a surprise pitch

Preserve the supplied item's silhouette, proportions, material, color,
graphic layout, controls, and authorized branding. Choose the most recognizable
presentation angle and leave its defining feature unobstructed by the grip.

A compact device, bottle, or package can face the viewer while its presenter
remains readable. Larger goods may use a stable display or a clear detail;
do not shrink them into arbitrary handheld miniatures simply to mimic the
source. The eventual offer layout should not cover the item's key feature.

Treat weather as scene treatment, not proof of waterproofing, impact resistance,
or safety. Keep any supported use demonstration separate from fictional
predicament staging. Price and bundle graphics are later campaign content;
they do not alter the identity asset or its packaging facts.

Create the requested clear asset reference, with enough material and shape
detail to remain recognizable after the rapid camera transition.
