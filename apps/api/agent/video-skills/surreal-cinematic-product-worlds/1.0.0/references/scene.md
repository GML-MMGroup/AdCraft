# Departure, threshold, and expanded space

Design an ordinary starting environment and a coherent transformed environment.
Differentiate them through depth, light, material, scale, or atmosphere while
retaining a shared spatial clue: horizon direction, central axis, opening,
surface plane, or a recognizable architectural relationship.

The reference contrasts an enclosed cool workspace with an open warm landscape.
Other briefs can use folded interiors, enlarged everyday geometry, liquid-like
surfaces, or another approved impossible rule. A field, office, and gold palette
are optional. The arrival should be understandable after a brief reveal.

Use the environment board to establish complementary views and useful geometry,
not near-identical decorative images. Cover each required spatial state in its
assigned board; indicate transition relationships through layout and perspective.
Keep sun or key-light direction, depth, and materials consistent within a state.

Impossible surroundings can retain believable surface response and scale cues.
Reserve clear visual space where a later interaction or closing composition
needs readability, without making every background element move at once.
