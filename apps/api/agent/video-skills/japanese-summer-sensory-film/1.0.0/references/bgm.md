# Air, ease, and a small lift

Propose an original instrumental accompaniment for the chosen route. Light
plucked strings, soft keys, a rounded bass line, or sparse gentle percussion
can support summer freshness. An active outdoor passage may have a buoyant
pulse; a quiet routine may use more space and a slower harmonic rhythm.

Build a simple entrance, a little lift through activity or use, and a settled
ending beneath the hero view. Leave room for small environmental and contact
sounds, and for any supplied speech. Avoid making every cut a musical accent
or overwhelming a subtle human response with a triumphant climax.

Adapt the arrangement to the approved duration and audio mode. Any desired
synchronization is an editing intention, not an already executed mix. The
source recording's melody, tempo, instruments, and rights were not established;
this guidance proposes a compatible score without reproducing that recording.
