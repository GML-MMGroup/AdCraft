# Collision Montage

The commercial is built from cuts. Every pair of images collides into an idea
the audience completes, and the product is the idea they arrive at.

## Why It Works

The audience is not told the value; they infer it. Inference creates
ownership, and a sequence of strong collisions gives the brand a recognizable
editing signature.

## How To Execute

1. Name the product value as one shared quality.
2. Collect images from unrelated worlds that share that quality strongly.
3. Pair them so each cut produces a clear third idea.
4. Build rhythm and speed toward one decisive pairing.
5. End with the product as the image that completes the pattern.
