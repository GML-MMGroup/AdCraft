# Scale Shift

The commercial changes the size of the camera, not the size of the claim. At
the right scale, the product's work becomes something the audience can see.

## Why It Works

Many product benefits happen too small or too large to be noticed. Moving to
the scale where they happen turns an abstract promise into a visible event.

## How To Execute

1. Identify the scale at which the product actually does its work.
2. Choose a familiar human moment to start from.
3. Design one clear transition into the hidden scale.
4. Show the product acting at that scale with a single discovery.
5. Return to human scale so the insight connects to everyday life.
