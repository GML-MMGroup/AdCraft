# Accident Bullet-Time Commercial

Use a contact-triggered speed ramp for parallax-led product inspection.
Preserve the approved story, inventory, identities and medium.

## Deliver the complete action

Carry this chain into the actual narrative and provider prompt, not just the
handoff: incidental shoulder contact briefly tilts the upper torso slightly
backward relative to the hips, hands reflexively open, and the same supported
belongings depart into short outward/upward arcs. They remain airborne through
connected inspection and pullback; gravity resumes before their first landing.
Keep the backward direction explicit when condensing. A yielding knee supports
that modest recoil rather than replacing it with unspecified imbalance.

Keep the first-frame footwear reveal through all objects landing in one video
segment, within the fifteen-second cap and confirmed total. Reserve two to three
seconds for landing. Later story or payoff follows the grounded state.

## Establish cause and transfer

Track backward at footwear height and rise within the same take. Establish
ordinary use first: eyewear on the face, drink in a hand, accessories at real
supports, unless approved handling differs. Choose independent passing routes.
At one contact plane, shoulders/upper arms visibly touch; fabric compression and
the immediate small backward deflection establish the impulse. The anonymous
extra keeps their destination without a face introduction or avoidance detour.

Keep contact and the brief asymmetric recovery near normal speed. Show each
original clearing its support, with a visible gap and vacant source: opening
fingers and departing cup, glasses clearing nose/ears, accessory leaving its
resting point. Follow outward/upward momentum before strong slowing. Derive
height from support; move the camera to the item. Enter the first inspection
promptly instead of holding a staged recoil or slowly levitating belongings.

## Traverse one frozen space

Use the product plus two distinct supporting items unless the approved set
supplies those slots. Stage approved A/B/C near to far in a bounded depth corridor
with support-derived height and lateral offsets. Advance continuously: the
outgoing near edge grows past the lens while the already visible smaller next
item enlarges. Accelerate between visits, slow for detail, and continue with
connected roll/tilt and offsets. Give the product the strongest view. Only the
final pullback retreats; then resume gravity and finish settling.

## Preserve states across views

Freeze body, head and gaze, object geometry, closures and displaced material in
world space. Each original exists once; its vacated support stays empty in every
later body view and pullback. References supply identity/design while the story
owns possession and location. Flag stale conflicting media for replacement.
A splash needs an open source; a drop is not durability proof.

The grid is an off-screen story-process reference, not a first/last-frame target
or required cut list. Each panel is one instant with one location per original.
All inspection views remain airborne even when a design reference rests on a
surface. The final panel alone shows the settled result. Start Video directly
in the full-frame scene. Preserve approved speech and subtitle controls.
