# Character appearance

Keep the approved facial identity, age, silhouette, clothing, and rendering
medium consistent. Favor readable eyes, restrained natural expressions, and
wardrobe that belongs to the selected everyday setting. Soft side modeling
and muted fabric can support tension without turning a person into a villain.

The release can soften facial tension and brighten the same clothing; it
does not require a replacement face or a transformation. Neutral design and
turnaround references retain clear, even visibility rather than hiding the
subject in dramatic darkness. Do not add a doctor costume, clinical stereotype,
fright mask, or exaggerated distress when not part of the approved design.
