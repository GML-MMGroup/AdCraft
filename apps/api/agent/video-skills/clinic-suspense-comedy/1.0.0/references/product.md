# Recognition before dramatic treatment

Show the complete supplied shape, components, packaging and brand details
in reference assets, with accurate color and clear material separation. A
suspenseful film does not require a dark or partially obscured source image.
For a service, preserve its approved interface or brand identifier.

Favor surface detail that makes the confirmed benefit easy to read in the
final still. Avoid generic gloss, condensation or a blue-liquid appearance
copied from the source. Reference assets stay complete for production, while
the film reserves their first appearance for the final brand frame. Narrative
concealment should not obscure or damage these standalone references.
