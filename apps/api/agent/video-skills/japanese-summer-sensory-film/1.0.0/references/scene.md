# A coherent summer environment

Choose the setting from the approved occasion: a clear shoreline, a shaded
park path, a sports resting area, a balcony, or a room with airy window light.
Use directional daylight, readable shade, warm natural surfaces, and depth.
Blue water and palm foliage suit the reference but are not universal features.

Keep sun angle, horizon height, ground texture, spatial scale, and wind direction
compatible across views. Allow reflective highlights and translucent edges
without losing environmental detail. Quietness can come from open space and
simple layering rather than making the location empty or featureless.

In the required environment board, vary useful spatial coverage: approach,
wide layout, reverse view, shaded area, relevant surface, overhead relationship,
and close environmental texture as needed. Preserve one location while making
the views complementary. Temporal cues such as ripples, leaf movement, or
changing curtain light can later animate those established spaces.
