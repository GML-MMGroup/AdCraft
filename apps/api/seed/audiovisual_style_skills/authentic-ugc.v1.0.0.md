---
skill_id: authentic-ugc
version: 1.0.0
skill_kind: audiovisual_style
title: Authentic UGC
objective: >
  Make the advertisement read like something a real person filmed on their own.
visual_principles: >
  Phone-camera point of view, natural light, home and everyday settings, and
  deliberately unpolished framing.
camera: >
  Slight handheld motion, imperfect composition, and natural movement rather
  than locked-off studio setups.
product_treatment: >
  The product is handled the way a real owner handles it: held, used, opened,
  and shown at arm's length instead of staged on a plinth.
editing: >
  Reach the point quickly, jump cuts are allowed, and everyday traces such as
  imperfect timing or background activity are kept.
sound: >
  Voice first, ambient sound preserved, and music kept low or absent.
suitable_cases: >
  Everyday consumer goods, social-first launches, and categories where polish
  would undercut credibility.
---

# Authentic UGC

The credibility comes from imperfection that is chosen on purpose. Polish that
creeps back in is what makes the format stop working.
