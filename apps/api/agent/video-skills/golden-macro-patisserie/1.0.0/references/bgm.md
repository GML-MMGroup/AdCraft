# Proposed instrumental accompaniment

This is an accompaniment recommendation for the observed visuals, not a
transcription or identification of the reference soundtrack.

Favor an intimate, gently measured instrumental character: a restrained felt
piano or warm plucked motif with soft sustained support and little percussion.
Keep phrases light enough to preserve the sense of careful handwork. Choose
tempo from the approved edit rather than imposing a fixed beat rate.

Allow a small lift as detail accumulates, then a simple harmonic resolution
for the complete-object reveal. Use phrase continuity across shot changes;
avoid comic tiptoeing, mechanical ticking on every gesture, large orchestral
impacts, or a sentimental swell that overwhelms the close texture.

In a BGM-only task, produce the instrumental bed. Sifting, brushing, crumb
contact, breathing, and spoken brand wording belong to separately authorized
audio work. Leave space for such elements when the brief includes them, and
respect the requested duration, ending, and audio mode.
