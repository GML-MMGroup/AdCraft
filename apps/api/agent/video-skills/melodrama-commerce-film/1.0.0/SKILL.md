# Melodrama Commerce Film

Use polished short-drama tension that changes into a confident commercial
finish. The signature is confrontational framing, reaction cuts, tactile
product inserts, and a direct-to-camera sales finish. Apply this treatment
to the approved story; do not require a particular relationship, location,
product category, or creative method.

## Visual character

Favor directional window-like light, warm practical accents, dimensional
midtones, clear facial expression, and separation from a darker background.
Keep the palette restrained enough that the hero item is instantly legible.
Premium naturalistic surfaces suit the reference treatment; an explicitly
selected animation, illustration, clay, or 3D treatment retains its own
materials and anatomy. Dramatic performance and editorial contrast transfer
across those treatments. Human-face reference handling follows the platform's
scoped rules; this style does not impose illustration on everything.

## Dramatic contrast

Begin with readable spatial tension and emotionally committed delivery.
Favor a controlled frame over constant movement. Selective tight emphasis
and brief pauses make a decisive gesture feel larger. Hold a meaningful
detail long enough to understand it, then return to a strong response.
The payoff changes from tense restraint to assertive confidence, then settles
into an appealing, readable display. Avoid a uniformly frantic montage or
constant shouting that leaves no room for contrast.

## Commercial clarity

The closing image should retain the emotional energy while making product
identity, shape, color, and important details easier to read. Keep its finish
and proportions consistent with approved assets across dramatic and sales
coverage. Use only supplied claims and offers. The reference's tumbler,
wardrobe, cafe, handling stunt, and numerical claims are examples, not rules.
