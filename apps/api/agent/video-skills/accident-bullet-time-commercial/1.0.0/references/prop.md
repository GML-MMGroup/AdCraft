# Props explain the force and the destination

Choose only props required by the approved cause: a carried tray, strap, curb,
loose paper, or landing towel. Specify how the prop supports or contacts the
hero and how that support changes. Distinguish an advertised item from a context
prop so a cup or bag does not become an unrequested second product.

Reference images should establish stable dimensions, attachment points and
material. Keep a handle, strap or rope continuous and usable. Give a tray a
credible rim and load arrangement; a curb needs a visible catching edge.
Maintain one instance of every prop through the event. A few droplets or crumbs
may reveal motion, but they need a real source and must not obscure the hero.
Avoid adding a dog, shopping bag or phone just because it appears in a source.
