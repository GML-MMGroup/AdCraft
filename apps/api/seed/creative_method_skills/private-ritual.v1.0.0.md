# Private Ritual

The commercial follows one person out of the visible world and into a moment
that belongs only to them, with the product at the center of that moment.

## Why It Works

Recognition. The viewer has lived this exact moment and rarely sees it taken
seriously on screen.

## How To Execute

1. Establish the outer world quickly and concretely.
2. Mark the exit with one physical action.
3. Make the product behavior specific and unhurried.
4. Shift rhythm or sound as the private state arrives.
5. Keep the ending inside the private moment; do not return to the world.
