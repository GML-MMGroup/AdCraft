# Stable design through extraordinary scale

Record the supplied item's silhouette, proportions, materials, key colors,
closure or controls, graphic layout, and authorized branding. Maintain a clear
normal-scale identity reference. A planned hero enlargement changes scale,
not the relative proportions, package design, or material identity.

Separate the marketed item from its contents and environmental motifs. A snack
bag may remain stable while separate chips float; a jar can remain unchanged
while a texture landscape evokes its contents. Those motifs should not
silently replace the merchandise or establish unsupported ingredients.

Choose tactile details by category: the actual food surface, appropriate
liquid or cream behavior, fabric construction, or approved device interaction.
Retain the correct state after opening, contact, use, or a deliberate break.
A macro view needs a traceable relation to the supplied item; it is not evidence
for unverified scientific, nutritional, origin, or performance claims.

Present the asset in its assigned reference format. The surreal setting is a
later staging choice, not a reason to distort the core design.
