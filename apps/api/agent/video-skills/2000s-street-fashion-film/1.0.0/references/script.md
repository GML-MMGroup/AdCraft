# An urban fashion vignette

Turn the approved attitude into a light sequence of entry, movement, observation,
and arrival. Let a visible look and a specific detail recur between street beats.
A model photo and brand name are a starting input, not a limit on the cast.
Use the approved solo, pair, or ensemble and distinguish who wears or carries
each hero item.

For a 30-second brief, reserve roughly the final few seconds for recognition
of the exact brand name. Distribute the rest between a quick introduction,
wearing and material moments, and an urban progression. Change those allocations
to fit the actual brief. Do not turn the source edit into a compulsory shot list.

Let a look, step, change of grip, or shared glance carry the story without
requiring narration. Preserve dialogue or voiceover when requested. Avoid
inventing an argument merely to justify a fashion montage. Deliver exact brand
wording and optional approved collection wording separately from the images, with
placement, legibility time, and ending intent in the production handoff.
