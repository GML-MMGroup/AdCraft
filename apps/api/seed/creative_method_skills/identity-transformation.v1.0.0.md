# Identity Transformation

The commercial is built around one switch. Everything before it belongs to
state A, everything after it belongs to state B, and the product performs the
switching.

## Why It Works

The viewer does not only see a product benefit; the viewer sees a version of
themselves on the other side of the trigger.

## How To Execute

1. Define state A concretely with behavior, sound, and space.
2. Place the product trigger at the exact hinge of the story.
3. Mark the transition through action, mirror, space, or sound.
4. Land state B with enough texture that the identity is legible.
5. Never let the product appear only as a prop after the change.
