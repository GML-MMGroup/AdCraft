# Supporting belongings complete the required camera tour

One advertised product still needs two distinct supporting belongings for the
three-item freeze and camera traversal. Choose context-appropriate items from
the approved story, such as eyewear, a neutral drink cup, phone or loose shoulder
bag. The categories can change with the setting; their separate visit slots
cannot. These are story props, not alternate packaging, duplicate SKUs or new
advertised products. Keep competing logos and unsupported selling claims out.

Give each supporting item its own stable identity and usable reference through
the normal prop-design workflow. Specify real dimensions, material, grip/strap
support, open/closed state, a feature for its close view and its eventual landing
state. Keep holes, handles and attachment points structurally continuous. Every
item must be visibly carried or worn before the collision and have a plausible
release; the frozen interval never introduces a previously unseen belonging.

Reference images show reusable isolated geometry in the current asset format,
not the full accident. Maintain one physical instance of each prop. Liquid or
crumb effects need a real source and must remain in the same frozen state during
the tour. A sealed container retains its closure and contents; condensation on its
exterior is compatible with sealing. Opening or a spill requires the actual
mechanism and an approved action before freeze.
