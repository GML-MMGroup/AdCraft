# Instrumental tension and release

Begin with sparse, low-density instrumental texture: a restrained pulse,
low sustained tone, or small unresolved motif. Leave room for speech and the
approved product cue. Build anticipation through spacing and harmonic tension
rather than a fixed tempo, continuous riser, or loud horror stinger.

At the approved reveal timing, a short score rest or decisive harmonic change
can open into a lighter texture. Resolve the motif with warmer harmony,
cleaner articulation, or a relaxed groove suited to the brand. A calm product
may need a quiet resolution rather than a cheerful jingle. Keep the final
callback intelligible and provide a deliberate close at the requested length.

Produce instrumental accompaniment only. Dialogue, whispers, breathing,
heartbeats, product noises, clicks, bubbling, and laughter are not BGM assets.
If exact transition timing is not supplied, propose the cue position for
authoring; do not claim synchronization that the current render or editing
contract cannot express. Respect the project's selected audio mode.
