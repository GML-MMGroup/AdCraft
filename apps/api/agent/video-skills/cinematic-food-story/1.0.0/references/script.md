# Sensory writing for the approved idea

Use ingredient texture, process progression, steam and sound detail, and an
appetite-led reveal as available sensory techniques. A preparation sequence
belongs here only when already part of the concept; a finished food, shared
meal, joke or quiet use moment can carry the same appetizing treatment.

Describe selected tactile changes and their audible contact. When showing a
process, preserve intermediate states needed to understand it. Choose emphasis,
pauses and delivery for the approved plot. Narration adds supplied facts or
relevant feeling, with no invented provenance or manufacturing claim. Keep the
actual product encounter and confirmed ending intact.
