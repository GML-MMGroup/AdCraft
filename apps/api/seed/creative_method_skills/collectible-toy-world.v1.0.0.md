# Collectible Toy World-Building

The commercial turns the brand subject into a collectible figure and gives
that figure a complete, coherent world to live in.

## Why It Works

Collectibility is a complete emotional system: recognition, desire, pride, and
display. A toy world invites the audience to own the brand, not only watch it.

## How To Execute

1. Reduce the subject to a silhouette recognizable from a distance.
2. Choose one to three signature accessories and repeat them.
3. Explore material charm: resin, plush, translucent plastic, metal.
4. Build the world around the toy so it belongs somewhere.
5. Keep audiovisual style techniques as separate style skills, not here.
