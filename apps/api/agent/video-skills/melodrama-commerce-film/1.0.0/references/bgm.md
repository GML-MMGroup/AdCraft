# Proposed tension-to-confidence accompaniment

This is an adaptable score proposal, not a transcription of the source track.
Use an instrumental dramatic bed with a sparse tension pulse and room for
dialogue. A restrained low-register motif, muted percussion, or short bowed
texture can establish pressure without turning every exchange into a climax.

Build toward the approved decisive action, then use a brief gap or release
at the reversal. Move into a clearer, more confident rhythmic phrase for
the sales passage; finish with a compact resolving accent under the stable
product hold. Align transitions with supplied edit landmarks rather than
inventing a fixed tempo or shot duration.

Keep dialogue intelligible through the final benefit and CTA. Avoid vocals
that compete with speech, continuous impact hits, and an unresolved ending.
Do not include dialogue, environmental ambience, or object foley in the
instrumental asset. Follow the chosen audio mode and soundtrack ownership;
a silent brief does not acquire music merely by selecting this style.
