# Environment treatment

Create an empty architectural environment with warm cream surfaces, one soft
pink or powder-toned accent, and clear blue atmospheric depth. Describe the
environmental palette directly from the approved world brief.

Favor open circulation, curved paths, rounded pavilions, pale plazas, clear
glazing, restrained metallic trim, and distant skyline or landscape anchors.
A waterfront or leisure-park setting is one option. Adapt the architecture to
the selected location without inventing a new setting or a mandatory landmark.

Define adjacent spaces, genuine openings, floor levels, sightlines, and fixed
structural positions. Preserve that layout across views. Separate a nearby
framing edge, usable middle space, and a distant horizon; leave the central
staging area visually quiet.

Use consistent clear daylight and broad sky fill. Make glass thickness,
painted panels, stone or paving, and metal reflections distinguishable.
Maintain readable shadow direction and plausible contact shading.

Describe static environment views with the same architecture and atmosphere.
Keep the image useful as a clean spatial reference for later staging.
