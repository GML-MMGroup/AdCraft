# Restrained period support

Choose a few readable period objects with tangible wood, lacquer, ceramic,
or metal surfaces and believable fabrication. Reflect hierarchy through
finish and ornament, without making every small object compete for attention.

Keep the requested isolated prop consistent in scale, color, wear, and
construction. A serving tray, desk accessory, or ceremonial furnishing is
optional and must follow the brief. Do not automatically add a throne,
weapons, food, or unrelated brand marks.
