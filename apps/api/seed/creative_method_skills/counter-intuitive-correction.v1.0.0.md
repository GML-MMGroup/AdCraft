# Counter-Intuitive Correction

This method converts a widely held false belief into the engine of the story.
The commercial earns attention by disagreeing with the viewer in the first
seconds, then repays that tension with a product-anchored correction.

## Why It Works

The viewer arrives with a prediction. Breaking that prediction creates a gap
that demands resolution, and resolution that arrives through the product makes
the product the author of the new understanding.

## How To Execute

1. Name the default belief the audience actually holds.
2. Challenge it visually in the opening seconds without narration.
3. Show the consequence of the old belief.
4. Let the product enter as the explanation or the proof.
5. Close on the corrected understanding, not on a slogan.
